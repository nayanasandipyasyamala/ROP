## 3. Key decisions and constraints

1. **ROP-VL (Shenzhen Eye Hospital (SZEH), China)**: 2020 images, 1116 patients. Laser-treated images (345) are excluded from Task A. Severe disease (Stage 4, Stage 5, A-ROP): 237 images from 158 patients; Stage 5: 30 images from 27 patients. The paper names three devices but gives no per-image device, so the size groups ({'G3_>1200': 1341, 'G1_<900': 601, 'G2_900-1200': 78}; patients with images in >1 group: 8 of 1116) are inferred acquisition groups, not devices.
2. **Ostrava (University Hospital Ostrava, Czech Republic)**: 6004 images, 188 patients, 484 exams, three devices with image sizes {'Clarity RetCam 3': '640x480', 'Phoenix ICON': '1240x1240', 'Natus RetCam Envision': '1440x1080'}. images read from images_stack_without_captions (no burned-in captions). Task A: Normal 2980 images (135 patients) vs ROP stages 1-3 835 images (17 patients); 5 patients have both labels. AP-ROP (470 images) comes from 1 patient(s) and is excluded from the main analysis. Labels are per exam, not per image.
3. **HVDROPDB (PBMA's H.V. Desai Eye Hospital, Pune, India)**: 185 classification images (Neo normal/ROP 50/35, RetCam-sized normal/ROP 50/50). No patient IDs, so it is used for external testing only. Duplicate evidence: not assessed in this module
4. **FARFUM-RoP**: 1533 images, 68 patients. Labels grade plus disease (normal 782 / pre-plus 479 / plus 272); they do not indicate ROP presence, so it is a side task only.
5. **Shenzhen 2024**: 1099 images. EXCLUDED from the main experiments: same hospital and period as ROP-VL and near-duplicates found; not independent. Evidence: Separate analysis: 65 of 1,099 Shenzhen images were within pHash distance <= 4 of an ROP-VL image (482 within <= 8); 24 pairs had correlation >= 0.98. Looser thresholds are not informative.
